\section{Results and interpretation}
\label{sec:results}

\paragraph{RQ1: contamination rate.}
Table~\ref{tab:accuracy} and Fig.~\ref{fig:results} show a monotonic decline for
every corruption type in the ten-model means. Mixed accuracy fell from 87.77\%
at 10\% contamination to 73.65\% at 30\%; systematic accuracy fell from
86.67\% to 70.03\%; and unseen-class accuracy fell from 93.61\% to 90.17\%.
The corresponding 30\% gaps from clean training were 22.57, 26.19, and 6.05
percentage points. Individual runs were not perfectly monotonic: the full
10--20--30\% ordering appeared in nine mixed, nine systematic, and seven unseen
repetitions. The pooled trend is therefore clear, but a single training run can
hide it.

\paragraph{RQ2: contamination type.}
Mixed and systematic errors were much more damaging than unseen-class
contamination. Systematic accuracy was lower than mixed accuracy at 10\% and
30\%, but mixed was lower by 0.30 points at 20\%. This small reversal does not
support a fixed ranking between the two target-label error types. Both attach
target features to an incorrect target label. The unseen images were balanced
across labels, so they created no stable link to one target output.

The systematic confusion matrix gives more direct evidence. With 30\%
contamination, 1,227 of 5,000 airplane predictions were trucks, 1,007 monkey
predictions were airplanes, and 1,685 truck predictions were monkeys. These
imposed directions account for 3,919 of 4,496 errors (87.2\%). The models
therefore learned much of the incorrect cyclic structure.

\paragraph{Training variation.}
The clean-model standard deviation was 0.63 percentage points. Variation was
largest for systematic 30\% and mixed 20\% contamination (6.17 and 6.08
points). Systematic 30\% model means ranged from 58.87\% to 79.33\%. One
downloaded model would therefore give a weak estimate of the contamination
effect.

\paragraph{Limits of the comparison.}
The first three repetitions share fixed pools, whereas the last seven resample
training and test data. Teachable Machine hides the training seed, and
replacement noise also removes correct examples. Results may not generalize
beyond this architecture, these six STL-10 classes, and the tested rates.
